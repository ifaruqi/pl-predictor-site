What you're looking at

For every Premier League match: the probability of a home win, a draw and an away win, each team's expected goals, and the chance of every exact score. Predictions are refreshed every day as results come in.

How to read it

  • Probabilities, not certainties. A 60% favourite fails to win about four times in ten.
  • The score pick ("if Chelsea win: 2–1") is the most likely score if the most likely result happens. Exact scores are hard: even the best pick comes true only about one time in nine. The single most likely score overall is 1–1 in most matches, because a draw has few ways to happen and they bunch up there, while a win is spread across many scores.
  • Expected goals is the average number of goals a team would score if the match were played many times.

How good is it?

Every prediction is saved before kick-off and scored afterwards; see Track record. In tests on more than 2,700 past Premier League matches, the predictions were close to the bookmakers' in accuracy.
